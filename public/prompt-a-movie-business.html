  1. Enter your prompt — describe your film, music video, or concept

  4. Central Casting — canonical character profiles + identity enforcement
  5. Fountain Screenplay — script aligned to roadmap

  7. Assembly — FFmpeg assembly into a finished cut with titles and credits
  8. Stream or Download — your film is ready
