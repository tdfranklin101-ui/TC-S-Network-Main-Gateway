The Current-See, PBC, Inc. and TC-S Network Foundation, Inc. Announce Dual Launch of Promptly and AI-Native Solar Economy & Marketplace Network
Dual launch introduces an AI-native film studio and a solar economy platform supporting the long-term development of a Global Basic Income Network
SAN FRANCISCO, Calif., April 24, 2026 — The Current-See, PBC, Inc. and TC-S Network Foundation, Inc. today announced a dual launch of Promptly, an AI-native agentic film studio, and the TC-S Network Solar Economy & Marketplace Network, an AI-native platform designed to support the long-term development of a Global Basic Income Network.
The launch brings forward two connected initiatives. The Current-See, PBC, Inc. is the commercial entity developing the network infrastructure, AI systems, and marketplace tools that power Promptly and the broader TC-S platform. TC-S Network Foundation, Inc. is the mission-driven entity using that network to advance the buildout of a Global Basic Income Network.
Promptly is designed as an AI-native film studio that transforms a single prompt into a treatment, screenplay, scene plan, casting logic, render pathway, and production workflow through coordinated AI agents. The platform is intended to compress the path from concept to execution for creators, studios, and production teams.
TC-S Network Foundation, Inc. serves as custodian of the Solar reserve and is focused on applying the TC-S network toward solar-based economic participation and marketplace access. Within the system, 1 Solar is defined as 4,913 kilowatt-hours, and 8.5 billion Solar have been minted per day since April 7, 2025. As of this announcement, the reserve stands at approximately S3.2 trillion.
Both organizations are AI-native and utilize the Unified Intelligence Mesh together with Replit agentic coding to accelerate development, deployment, automation, and continuous system improvement.
“This dual launch reflects two aligned missions,” said Terry Franklin, Founder of The Current-See and TC-S Network Foundation. “One is to expand human creative capacity through AI-native production tools. The other is to build the economic and marketplace architecture for a future Global Basic Income Network.”
The organizations said the dual launch marks an early but foundational step in the development of infrastructure intended to support both advanced creative production and broader economic participation through AI-coordinated systems.
About The Current-See, PBC, Inc.
The Current-See, PBC, Inc. is an AI-native public benefit corporation developing commercial infrastructure, agentic software systems, marketplace tools, and creative production platforms, including Promptly, an AI agentic film studio.
About TC-S Network Foundation, Inc.
TC-S Network Foundation, Inc. is an AI-native mission-driven organization advancing the development of a Global Basic Income Network using the TC-S platform. The Foundation serves as custodian of the Solar reserve and supports the long-term buildout of solar-based economic participation.
