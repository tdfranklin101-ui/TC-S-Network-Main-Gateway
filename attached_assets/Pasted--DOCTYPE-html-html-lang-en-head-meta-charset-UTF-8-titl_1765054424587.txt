<!-- APPENDIX A -->
<details>
<summary>Appendix A — Energy Sector Deployment</summary>
<p>
<strong>Overview:</strong> A renewable-energy provider deploys the TC-S OS to create an autonomous micro-economy aligned with renewable generation.<br><br>

<strong>Issuance:</strong> Solar is issued to the region based on renewable input (kWh). It is internal, non-fiat, and governed by the Foundation.<br><br>

<strong>Autonomous Markets:</strong><br>
– Energy-aware pricing<br>
– Circular goods marketplace<br>
– Multi-agent optimization<br><br>

<strong>Robotics Integration:</strong> Robots manage grid maintenance, logistics, environmental monitoring, and coordinate tasks in Solar.<br><br>

<strong>Government Integration:</strong> Supports GBI pilots, energy incentive programs, sustainability alignment.<br><br>

<strong>Outcomes:</strong> Energy-aligned economic behavior, autonomous grid management, sustainability-based commerce, multi-agent coordination.
</p>
</details>

<!-- APPENDIX B -->
<details>
<summary>Appendix B — Retail & Autonomous Commerce</summary>
<p>
<strong>Overview:</strong> Retailers deploy TC-S to support autonomous stores, circular markets, AI valuation, and robotics inventory ecosystems.<br><br>

<strong>Identify Anything:</strong> Real-time item classification, condition scoring, kWh footprinting, and valuation in Solar.<br><br>

<strong>Autonomous Commerce:</strong><br>
– Unmanned stores<br>
– Solar-valued pricing<br>
– Consumer-to-consumer resale markets<br><br>

<strong>Robotics:</strong> Robots negotiate tasks, restock shelves, handle logistics, operate inspections, and integrate into multi-agent markets.<br><br>

<strong>Sustainability:</strong> kWh footprint pricing naturally rewards low-energy goods.<br><br>

<strong>Outcomes:</strong> Circular economy dominance, autonomous retail operations, sustainable consumption, multi-agent retail ecosystems.
</p>
</details>

<!-- APPENDIX C -->
<details>
<summary>Appendix C — Government & Sovereign Deployment</summary>
<p>
<strong>Overview:</strong> Governments deploy TC-S to build digital public infrastructure, sovereign-scale micro-economies, and GBI systems.<br><br>

<strong>Solar Issuance:</strong> Public programs receive Solar backed by renewable energy metrics and governance rules.<br><br>

<strong>GBI Marketplace:</strong> Transparent, energy-indexed distribution of essentials, transportation, education benefits, etc.<br><br>

<strong>Autonomous Public Services:</strong><br>
– Robotics for infrastructure tasks<br>
– AI agents for eligibility + distribution<br>
– Energy-aligned policy execution<br><br>

<strong>Strategic Outcomes:</strong> Non-fiat welfare, autonomous public systems, energy-aligned economic growth, sovereign digital transformation.
</p>
</details>

<!-- APPENDIX D -->
<details>
<summary>Appendix D — Robotics & Multi-Agent Systems</summary>
<p>
<strong>Overview:</strong> Robots use Solar as a shared economic grammar to negotiate tasks, optimize energy use, and coordinate autonomously.<br><br>

<strong>Multi-Agent Task Markets:</strong><br>
– Robots bid for tasks<br>
– Energy cost → Solar<br>
– Task value → Solar<br>
– Fleet-level optimizations emerge naturally<br><br>

<strong>Industries:</strong><br>
– Smart cities<br>
– Warehousing<br>
– Agriculture<br>
– Manufacturing<br>
– Defense (non-lethal systems)<br><br>

<strong>Identify Anything Integration:</strong> Robots classify objects, estimate effort, and compute the Solar-equivalent cost of completing tasks.<br><br>

<strong>Outcomes:</strong> Self-organizing fleets, energy-efficient operations, unified human-robot economic systems.
</p>
</details>
