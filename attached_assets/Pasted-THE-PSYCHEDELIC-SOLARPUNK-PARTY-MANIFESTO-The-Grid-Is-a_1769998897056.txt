The Grid Is a Lie You’ve Been Living Inside

And they convinced you that you can’t touch it. That you need their permission. Their infrastructure. Their monthly invoices for light that falls free from the sky.
This is the biggest con in human history, and you’ve been paying for it your whole life.
The Psychedelic Solarpunk Party exists because some of us woke up. We saw the pattern. We recognized the cage. And we realized the door was never locked—we just forgot how to open it.

Your battery is a bank account that compounds in photons. Your solar panel is a declaration of independence written in silicon and light.

You stop being a consumer and become a generator. You stop asking permission and start making decisions. You see the network differently. You see yourself differently. You see the sun differently. What was background radiation becomes active relationship.

This is how it spreads. Not through policy. Through perception.
