    <h1 style="text-align:center; margin-bottom: 40px;">TC-S Client Mosaic Examples</h1>

    <!-- ======================== -->
    <!-- EXAMPLE #1 -->
    <!-- ======================== -->
    <h2>1. A Data Company With Its Own Power-Generating Capabilities</h2>
    <p><strong>“Your power becomes your product. Your data becomes an economy.”</strong></p>

    <p>Imagine a hyperscale data company running AI clusters, edge compute nodes, or distributed GPU farms — but now it can 
    <strong>monetize its internal power generation</strong> (solar, geothermal, microgrid, waste-heat recapture) through its 
    <strong>own tokenized economy</strong>, automatically deployed with TC-S.</p>

    <h3>What they get:</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>A <strong>Data Power Token</strong> backed by the company’s own kilowatt-hour streams</li>
        <li>Automated wallets for tenants, clients, and compute customers</li>
        <li>Token-based metering for GPU time, storage, bandwidth, and power draw</li>
        <li>AI-managed load balancing and energy economics</li>
        <li>Internal marketplaces (compute resale, storage resale, priority access, GPU auctions)</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>In plain English:</strong>  
    The data company becomes both a <strong>power utility</strong> and a <strong>compute marketplace</strong>, with revenue moving instantly through tokens — 
    no invoicing, no net-15, no reconciliation.</p>

    <h3>Relatable Example:</h3>
    <p>A client buys 20 minutes of inference on a GPU cluster.  
    They scan a QR code; the system measures the kWh consumed; the payment settles instantly in the private Data Power Token.  
    No billing. No delay. No human admin.</p>

    <h3>Potential:</h3>
    <p>A fully autonomous digital utility model where energy + compute = currency.</p>

    <!-- ======================== -->
    <h2>2. Energy Storage Management (Large Battery Farms Sourced by Renewables)</h2>
    <p><strong>“Your battery becomes a bank. Every charge and discharge becomes revenue.”</strong></p>

    <p>A battery-farm operator needs to track:
        source-of-energy provenance, time-of-day pricing, cycling optimization, sellbacks, arbitrage windows, load-shifting, 
        and client billing.
        TC-S turns the entire operation into a <strong>tokenized energy bank</strong>, with every kWh entering or leaving the system automatically 
        logged, valued, priced, and transacted.
    </p>

    <h3>What they get:</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>A <strong>Battery Reserve Token</strong> tied directly to stored energy</li>
        <li>Smart wallets for customers buying storage capacity or discharge windows</li>
        <li>AI predicting optimal charge/discharge cycles</li>
        <li>Automated REC tracking</li>
        <li>Revenue splits between generator, storage operator, and commissioner</li>
        <li>Real-time dashboards showing power flows and token flows simultaneously</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Relatable Example:</h3>
    <p>A solar farm wants to store 5 MWh overnight.  
    They buy “Reserve Tokens” for their share of the storage window.  
    When the stored energy is sold to the grid at peak, the token value automatically adjusts and revenue splits trigger without human intervention.</p>

    <h3>Potential:</h3>
    <p>A storage operator becomes a <strong>digital energy clearinghouse</strong> with its own micro-economy and automated settlement engine.</p>

    <!-- ======================== -->
    <h2>3. Public Utilities (Renewable, Nuclear, Hydro, Natural Gas, Mixed Grid)</h2>
    <p><strong>“A unified digital layer for every source of power you produce.”</strong></p>

    <p>Public utilities manage multiple energy sources, fluctuating grid demand, customer billing across millions, maintenance cycles, 
    subsidies, credits, renewables mandates, and demand response programs.</p>

    <p>TC-S overlays the utility with a <strong>unified digital token economy</strong> where each energy type can have its own economic rules, 
    while still rolling up into a single customer experience.</p>

    <h3>What they get:</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>A <strong>Utility Token</strong> (or multiple strata tokens: Renewables Token, Baseline Token, Nuclear Token, etc.)</li>
        <li>Smart wallets for households and businesses</li>
        <li>Instant energy purchases, pay-as-you-go models, or prepaid energy blocks</li>
        <li>AI-driven demand-response incentives</li>
        <li>Automated REC and carbon-credit settlement</li>
        <li>Ability to issue event-, region-, or outage-specific micro-tokens to stabilize local markets</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Relatable Example:</h3>
    <p>During a heat wave:  
    Utility issues “Peak Relief Tokens” to incentivize customers to lower consumption temporarily.  
    The AI agent monitors load and automatically distributes micro-rewards to customers who reduce usage.  
    Grid stabilizes faster, without manual coordination.</p>

    <h3>Potential:</h3>
    <p>A public utility becomes a <strong>dynamic, real-time, AI-managed energy marketplace</strong> instead of a slow, invoice-based billing institution.</p>

    <h2>4. A Solar-Powered Health Food Grocery Store</h2>
    <p><strong>“Your rooftop sun becomes store credit, customer loyalty, and community wealth.”</strong></p>

    <p>Imagine a neighborhood grocery store that runs on its own solar power. Now imagine that store turning its sunlight into a 
    <strong>customer-powered economy</strong> — rewarding shoppers, stabilizing margins, and tracking sustainability 
    in real time through a private token that the store owns.</p>

    <h3>What They Get</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>A branded token (e.g., <strong>SUNFOOD Tokens</strong>) backed by rooftop solar output</li>
        <li>Smart wallets for every shopper</li>
        <li>QR-coded pricing for every product</li>
        <li>Automated loyalty rewards powered by solar production</li>
        <li>Real-time sustainability reporting</li>
        <li>AI inventory, price, and demand guidance</li>
        <li>Integrated energy and REC insights</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>Relatable Example:</strong>  
    A shopper buys produce, kombucha, and grains.  
    At checkout, they scan a QR code.  
    Their wallet shows SUNFOOD total cost, tax included, and solar-earned rewards.  
    On sunny days, shoppers earn more because the roof produces more power.</p>

    <h3>Potential:</h3>
    <p>The store becomes a solar-powered loyalty ecosystem and community micro-economy.</p>

        <li>QR-coded pricing for all food, drinks, merch, art, and services</li>
        <li>Offline-friendly transactions</li>
        <li>AI moderation of supply/demand, flow, vendor restock, live economics</li>
        <li>Automated payouts and revenue splits</li>
        <li>Optional renewable microgrid + REC integration</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>Relatable Example:</strong>  
    Attendees receive FEST Wallets at the gate. They buy tacos, clothing, coffee, glitter, repairs — all by scanning QR codes.  
    The economy runs smoothly without POS systems or WiFi failures.  
    At sunset, a solar microgrid peaks and distributes extra FEST tokens to every attendee — a magical, communal moment.</p>

    <h3>Potential:</h3>
    <p>A renewable-powered instant city and circular economy that lives on year after year.</p>
